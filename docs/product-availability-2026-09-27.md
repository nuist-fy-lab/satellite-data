# Product catalog and availability audit

Snapshot: 2026-09-27 13:17 UTC, c2ln1. The complete 808-row, 34-column
catalog is in `product-catalog-2026-09-27.csv` (808 unique product keys).
It includes CloudSat for reference, but excludes it from the availability
checks below. The other 12 sources contain 784 product entries.

The CSV is a read-only snapshot of the centre's `products`, `catalog_sources`,
`official_days`, and `local_slots` tables, joined by product key to completed
downloader tasks. `catalog_refreshed_utc` is the last successful upstream
catalog refresh, not the last verification of an individual file. Empty
declared dates mean that the provider supplied no per-product date bounds;
they do not mean unlimited availability. `indexed_days` and `local_ok_slots`
are historical counts, not live upstream guarantees. `completed_files` is
evidence for that exact product key only, not for every date or file in it.
`query_evidence` distinguishes an indexed asset from a day that was checked
and found empty; `download_evidence` is based on completed tasks, not on a
claim that the product is currently accessible. Of 158 indexed product-days,
53 contained official assets and 105 were marked `official_missing`.
No token, account, signed URL, or local archive path is included.

## Source-level probes

Each satellite-source probe searched a narrow, known historical interval and
requested only bytes 0-255 of one freshly discovered asset through the centre
Unix gateway. A `206` with a matching `Content-Range` establishes a working
path for that asset at that time; it does not prove every product is accessible.
The completed-file column counts distinct product keys and downloader tasks
previously marked `done`, not new downloads from this audit.

The CALIPSO source URL resolves and an unauthenticated request redirects to
NASA Earthdata Login; HEAD redirects to a CloudFront distribution. The current
centre gateway returns 502 on its authenticated fetch. Its upstream error is
not exposed, so the available evidence does not distinguish a rejected token
from a disallowed redirect or another network failure. Do not broaden the
gateway redirect allowlist or forward bearer credentials to a CDN without
identifying the exact authorized transfer flow.

ERA5's six entries are CDS datasets generated by an asynchronous request,
not pre-existing files enumerated by the generic `/api/v1/search` route.
The centre has one enabled ERA5 account and reports an available route, but
the CDS job table has zero entries. Token validity, dataset licence acceptance,
job completion, and full-file download remain unverified. The current worker
does not automatically archive ERA5 results.

## Runtime incident

At approximately 2026-09-27 12:35 UTC, `central.sock`, `archive-events.sock`
and `downloader.sock` disappeared from their state directories while the
services remained active and retained their listening file descriptors.
Both centre and downloader requests to these socket paths then failed.
All 869 downloader tasks and 18 centre download requests were already `done`,
so both services were restarted in order. Their sockets, local health checks
and provider IPC recovered and were still present at 13:17 UTC. The process
that removed the socket pathnames has not been identified; the fault may recur.

## Interpretation

`808 catalog entries` does **not** mean `808 verified downloads`. Historical
successful full-file transfers cover only 12 product keys, and this audit
sampled one fresh asset per satellite source. The CSV deliberately leaves
unprobed product rows without a success claim. In particular, the 74 EarthCARE
Cal/Val or commissioning entries may require access beyond the account used
for the validated sample. CALIPSO currently fails, and ERA5 cannot yet be
claimed to have completed an end-to-end retrieval.

No project tests or builds ran on c2ln1 or RS. No new download tasks or CDS
requests were submitted. Only read-only catalogue searches and bounded
network-range probes were performed after the socket restart.
